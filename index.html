Generators

Small web pages that generate retro images: type your own text, fiddle with the settings, and save the result as a PNG. It all runs in the browser.

Split-flap departures board

An airport departures board. You can edit the flights, the title and the clock, pick white or yellow letters, choose black airport flaps or a railway-style ivory board, and turn on the clacking sound. Clicking the board flips everything again.

Sierra death dialog

The Sierra “You have died” box, drawn at 320×200 EGA. Two styles: AGI (white box, red border, “Press ENTER”) or early SCI (title bar and Restore / Restart / Quit buttons). Write your own cause of death, pick a background, and save it at up to 4× size.

Not built yet, in no particular order: a thermal till receipt, a Ceefax/teletext page, an arcade high-score table, a VHS rental sticker, LucasArts SCUMM dialog, and Sierra title screens. Planning notes are in the repository README.
