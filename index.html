Generators

Small web pages that generate retro images: type your own text, fiddle with the settings, and save the result as a PNG. It all runs in the browser.

Split-flap departures board

An airport departures board. You can edit the flights, the title and the clock, pick white or yellow letters, choose black airport flaps or a railway-style ivory board, and turn on the clacking sound. Clicking the board flips everything again.

Sierra death dialog

The Sierra “You have died” box, drawn at 320×200 EGA. Two styles: AGI (white box, red border, “Press ENTER”) or early SCI (title bar and Restore / Restart / Quit buttons). Write your own cause of death, pick a background, and save it at up to 4× size.

Thermal receipt

A till receipt from a store that does not exist. Edit the items and prices, set the tax, pay by cash or card, and toggle the loyalty points, the survey plug and the barcode. A fade slider ages the print until the thermal paper gives up.

Teletext page

A Ceefax-style page: 40×25 cells, seven colours, and every colour change spends a character cell, the way real teletext did. Double-height headlines, block graphics, coloured backgrounds, flashing text and the fastext row all work, and there is a scanline toggle for the full CRT effect. Comes with news, sport, weather, TV and market pages to start from.

Arcade high scores

The attract-mode score table from a game that never shipped. Name the game, pick the title colours, edit the best players and your 1UP score. INSERT COIN flashes, clicking the screen drops a coin in, and the CRT toggle adds glow, scanlines and a vignette.

Not built yet, in no particular order: a VHS rental sticker, LucasArts SCUMM dialog, and Sierra title screens. Planning notes are in the repository README.
