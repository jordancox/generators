On a phone you can also press and hold the GIF to save or copy it.

This copies the look of AnimatedText, the Tumblr where Cat Frazier (1991–2026) made spinning 3D text out of whatever people asked her for, using Xara 3D Maker.

The letters are real 3D in three.js, lit with old Phong shading and coloured lights. GIFs are encoded by gifski, which picks the colours across all the frames at once. Video comes out as MP4 where the browser can make one and WebM where it can't.
